# 📊 Tech Job Market Trends & AI Impact Dashboard

### Project Overview
With the tech industry experiencing massive shifts, I wanted to look beyond the headlines. I took a raw dataset of workforce trends and built a custom Power BI application to contrast layoff volumes with open hiring roles, explicitly tracking the impact of AI automation.

### 🌟 View the Dashboard
**[Click here to view the full Dashboard PDF](layoff_hiring.pdf)**

### 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Data Visualization:** Power BI
* **UI/UX Design:** Custom dark-mode interface, executive F-pattern layout.

### 💡 Key Insights
1. **The AI Impact:** AI Automation is driving a massive chunk of restructuring (over 22%), but it's simultaneously creating demand elsewhere.
2. **Where the Jobs Are:** While legacy roles are seeing cuts, Data Science and ML Engineering open roles are spiking.
3. **The Balance:** Layoffs and hiring are happening at the exact same time—it's a talent *shift*, not just a talent *loss*.
